Ogden Basic — Chinese learner edition

This curriculum is adapted from longlong-skyligo/Ogden by Skivein at the pinned revision:
https://github.com/longlong-skyligo/Ogden/tree/9d924597f047342b7cd78ba7ac928d940d3190ed

The source README describes Ogden as a secondary creation based on:
https://ogden.munch.love/
It also states that the original website author gave permission for that secondary creation.
The source repository supplies an MIT license. Its word data is 850 entries in five groups:
Operations (100), General Things (400), Picturable (200), Qualities (100), and Opposites (50).
The source README identifies the bundled word data, IPA data, and US/UK audio directories as:
app/src/main/assets/ogden_words.json
app/src/main/assets/ogden_ipa.json
app/src/main/assets/audio/us
app/src/main/assets/audio/uk

Mural retains stable word IDs and adapts the curriculum to native Swift. The app includes the
850 English words, Chinese meanings, definitions, examples, related words, IPA, and US/UK word
recordings from this pinned source. Four US spellings are displayed with the existing UK
spellings searchable. Twenty-seven entries have documented editorial changes for clarity,
grammar, sense alignment or translation. The imported English and Chinese text received
AI-assisted semantic screening; it has not been certified by a human dictionary editor.
Related words are not automatically interchangeable synonyms. IPA and individual pronunciations
have not all been independently reviewed. The source does not identify the recording speaker or
provider, and the recordings are not represented as OpenAI voices. Offline playback makes no
network request. Vietnamese explanations are generated live by the teacher.

The previous Mural word IDs and saved learning records remain readable. Earlier source material,
if present in a pre-update backup, is not relicensed by this notice.
