# Google Play purchase plan

Three consumable one-time products use the existing AI-value wallet, server verification,
durable receipt storage, consumption after credit, and refund reconciliation. The Play
build uses the Play Billing sheet; the separately signed direct APK keeps Stripe.

| Product ID | US price | Norway price | AI value credited in either market | Estimated conversation time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `chat.mural.android.minutes.small.v1` | $7 | NOK 89 | $3.69 | About 36.9 minutes |
| `chat.mural.android.minutes.medium.v1` | $13 | NOK 179 | $7.66 | About 76.6 minutes |
| `chat.mural.android.minutes.large.v1` | $20 | NOK 249 | $11.61 | About 116.1 minutes |

These are the iOS customer prices and AI allocations. The estimates use the existing
$0.10 per minute planning rate; actual use determines the debit. The six catalog rows
have distinct server SKUs but share three Play product IDs across USD and NOK. The
Android client selects the row matching Play's localized currency and exact price.

The [draft catalog](play-catalog-draft.json) is generated by
`node --import tsx ../../scripts/generate_play_catalog.mts live` from `services/api`.
It is **not approved for activation**. It assumes a 30% store commission, no US sales
tax in the catalog quote, 25% tax included in the Norway price, and a planning rate of
10 NOK per USD. These conservative assumptions preserve the iOS allocation at the
listed price. Before loading any Play rows into the protected production catalog,
confirm Google's actual service-fee enrollment, tax treatment, local product prices,
FX basis, product IDs, and the production pricing-policy version. Keep other billing
currencies unavailable until they have reviewed price and allocation rows.

Play's multi-quantity cart option stays **off**. The app can read and submit Google's
reported quantity, but the current server only verifies and credits one pack per
checkout. A shopper can make another purchase after the first is consumed. Enable
multi-quantity only after server verification, partial refunds, void handling, and
Play-installed recovery all pass for quantities up to ten.

The merchant profile is linked and the three products are saved as drafts in Play
Console. Production still lacks a Play service account, a purchase-binding key and
an approved catalog. Keep the purchase options inactive until those are configured
and a Play-installed build passes a license-tester purchase. Verify one credit,
consumption, balance use, restore and the support path before enabling sales.
