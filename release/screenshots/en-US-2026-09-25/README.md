# Mural 1.0 App Store screenshots

Eight 1320 × 2868 iPhone 17 Pro Max screenshots are uploaded to the English (U.S.) 6.9-inch App Store set in this order:

1. `01-conversation.png` — Spanish voice conversation and the listening orb
2. `05-mandarin.png` — Mandarin conversation with pinyin
3. `06-italian.png` — Italian conversation
4. `07-meaning.png` — word meaning sheet during a Spanish conversation
5. `02-themes.png` — everyday conversation themes
6. `03-words.png` — saved words and recall progress
7. `08-settings.png` — Settings, account entry and remaining guest time
8. `04-greeting.png` — opening conversation screen

The `originals/` directory holds simulator captures. The numbered files are opaque PNG exports with identical RGB pixels. They use the real app interface with local sample learning data and a simulated guest balance; they do not represent a live server session or an actual person's history. The screenshot fixture is available only in Debug simulator builds.
